Privacy policy

Last updated 28 August 2026

This policy covers the Freshers' Fortnight 2026 app at jcollegemcr.co.uk, run by the MCR committee of Jesus College, Cambridge.

What we collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and Google account identifier. We do not receive or request access to your Gmail, Drive, Calendar, contacts or any other Google service.

As you use the app we also store:

  • the display name you give the committee, if you choose to set one
  • which events you have signed up for, and your status for each
  • attendance records where a committee member checks you in at an event

Why we collect it

To run the Freshers' Fortnight programme: to allocate places at events with limited capacity, to manage waiting lists, to produce attendance lists for event organisers, and to contact you about events you have signed up for. We use it for nothing else.

Where it is stored

Data is held in a Google Sheet belonging to the Jesus College MCR, accessed through Google Apps Script. Access is restricted to members of the MCR committee who need it to run events.

Who we share it with

Nobody outside the MCR committee. We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or pass it to third parties. Where an event is held in a College venue, the committee may pass a list of attendee names to College staff where the venue or catering requires it.

How long we keep it

Event sign-up and attendance records are kept until the end of the academic year in which Freshers' Fortnight takes place, after which they are deleted. Your name and email are removed when you cease to be a member of the MCR.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Email the address below and we will act on it. You can also revoke this app's access to your Google account at any time from your Google account permissions page.

Cookies

The app stores a sign-in token in your browser so that you stay signed in between visits. There are no advertising or analytics cookies.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or about your data, go to george@jcollegemcr.co.uk, or write to Jesus College MCR, Jesus College, Jesus Lane, Cambridge CB5 8BL, United Kingdom.
