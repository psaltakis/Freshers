Freshers' Fortnight 2026

Sixteen days and forty five events, from 15 to 30 September, run by and for the graduate community at Jesus College, Cambridge.

What this app does

This site is the programme for Freshers' Fortnight. Members of the Jesus College MCR can browse every event, see an interactive map of the College grounds, put their name down for what they want to attend, and check what they have signed up for.

Signing in with a University of Cambridge Google account tells the committee who has registered for which event, so that places can be allocated fairly and attendance lists mean something.

Who runs it

The MCR (Middle Combination Room) is the graduate student body of Jesus College, Cambridge. This app is built and maintained by the MCR committee.

  • Jesus College, Jesus Lane, Cambridge CB5 8BL, United Kingdom
  • george@jcollegemcr.co.uk

Who can use it

Sign in is limited to current members of the Jesus College MCR using their University of Cambridge Google account. It is not a public service.
