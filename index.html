About the map

Who can open the hidden door

Administrators

Put an address on this list and that person is an administrator from the moment they sign in. They come straight in through the committee door whether or not anybody has added them to the roster, they land on the live desk, they see every detail of every event including who is coming and what they eat, and they can edit or cancel any of it. The president's address is on the list permanently.

The list

Anyone here can sign in through the committee door, edit the programme live, take charge of events and message the people going. Admins can also change this list.

Who is organising what

Everyone on the committee, with the events they are down to run. Their name and role appear on those event pages, so freshers know who they are turning up to meet.

Who counts as a fresher

By default anyone with a cam.ac.uk address can sign in. Paste the incoming freshers' CRSids or emails here, one per line, and only they get in.

The map's GPS

Stand at the Porters' Lodge, wait for a good fix, and press Calibrate. Everyone's dot is then corrected by the same amount.
