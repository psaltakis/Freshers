Administrators

An address on this list is an administrator from the moment they sign in, whether or not they are on the roster below. They see every event, every thread that is not welfare, and can edit any of it. The President's address is on the list permanently.

The roster

Anyone here is recognised as committee when they sign in. Their team decides where they appear on the People page, and a photograph makes a name into a person you can find.

Who may sign in

By default anyone with a cam.ac.uk address can sign in. Paste CRSids or addresses here, one per line, to limit access to those people.

JESUS COLLEGEThe MCR

Run by the MCR committee for the postgraduate community at Jesus College. The term calendar, the MCR room, the board, and who to contact. This is not an official Jesus College publication.

By signing in you agree to the MCR holding your name, your University address and what you put your name down for, under the UK GDPR.

Who holds it. The Jesus College MCR committee, as the data controller. The app runs on the MCR's own Google account and nothing is sold, shared with advertisers, or sent outside it.

What we hold. Your name and your University email address, taken from your Cambridge Google account when you sign in. After that: what you sign up for, what you put on the board, the room bookings you ask for, the threads you open, and how you answer a sounding. Nothing else. We never see or store a password.

Why we may hold it. Because you are a member of the MCR and running the MCR needs it: a list at a door, a room that is not double booked, and an answer that reaches the person who asked. Soundings are answered anonymously; the register keeps your address only so that one person cannot answer twice, and only counts and comments are ever read.

Who sees it. Other members see your name next to something you put up. The committee sees the running of it. A welfare thread is read by that one officer and the President, and by nobody else, and you may send it without your name at all.

How long. While you are a member, and for the academic year afterwards, after which it is cleared out. A welfare thread is cleared sooner.

Your rights. You may ask to see what is held about you, to have it corrected, or to have it deleted, and you may withdraw from anything you have said yes to. Write to president@mcr.jesus.cam.ac.uk and it will be dealt with within a month. If you are not satisfied you may complain to the Information Commissioner at ico.org.uk.

Use your @cam.ac.uk address. No new password needed.
