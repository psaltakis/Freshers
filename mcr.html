Settings

Your name

This is what appears on a list at a door and next to anything you put up. Everything else the app needs it already has from your University account.

Your calendar

Subscribe once and everything you put your name down for appears in your own calendar, with the alerts built in. It stays up to date on its own, and it is how your phone reminds you. The app emails only when something genuinely needs your inbox: a reply to something you asked, permission for a room, or a swap place waiting to be paid for.

The committee

Who can edit, and who serves what

Administrators

An address on this list is an administrator from the moment they sign in, whether or not they are on the roster below. They see every event, every thread that is not welfare, and can edit any of it. The president's address is on the list permanently.

The roster

Anyone here is recognised as committee when they sign in. Their team decides where they appear on the People page, and a photograph makes a name into a person you can find.

Who may sign in

By default anyone with a cam.ac.uk address gets in. Paste CRSids or addresses here, one per line, and only they do.

The house voice

Whoever writes in this app next will not have been in the room when it was built. These rules appear above every box anybody types into, which is the only way a register survives a handover.
