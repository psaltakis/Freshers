Jesus College MCR Soon Freshers' Fortnight

Coming up shortly

The Freshers' Fortnight app opens at this address in the next few days: the whole programme, your own list, the college map, and a place to put your name down for everything you would like to come to.

1 Keep this page Bookmark it, or add it to your Home Screen. The app appears here, at the same address.
2 Sign in with your University account You sign in with the Google account the University gave you, the one ending cam.ac.uk. Check your phone is signed in to that rather than a personal address and you will go straight in. There is nothing to set up and no password to make.
3 Check you are on the MCR register The app admits members of the Jesus MCR, and the register is also what tells it whether you are arriving this October or returning. If you are not on it yet, or it has you down as a returning member when you are a fresher, put it right now and the whole fortnight opens to you. Open the MCR register
4 Come back when it opens You will be able to see every event, read about it, and book a place. Some events have limited places, so it is worth coming early.

The fortnight begins Tuesday 15 September Welcome Drinks in the Brewery, at 7pm.
