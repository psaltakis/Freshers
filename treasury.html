Full ledger

The desk

President and Treasurer only

A spreadsheet holding every claim the MCR has, from which the whole job can be done: approve one, approve a smaller amount than was asked for, move a claim to a different budget line, put it to the college, turn it down, and tick it paid. Each of those does exactly what the button on this page does, and what you change there appears here on the next refresh.

The Treasurer is not on email at all. No payment requests, no claims to put to the Bursar, no reminders. All of it arrives on the desk instead, colour coded: amber for a claim waiting on the money, green once it is paid. Claimants still hear at every step, because they have nowhere else to look.

Committee

Roles are set on the server

People sign in with their own University account, and the address decides what they can do. Payment emails still go to the three shared committee inboxes below, so a change of treasurer does not change where anything lands. Both lists live in the Apps Script file, not in this page: to change either, edit ROLE_PEOPLE at the top of Code.gs and redeploy.

Who holds each role

Changes take effect at once

The inbox is where the treasury's mail goes and stays the same year to year. The people are the personal accounts allowed to sign in as that role, and change every handover. Several per role, comma separated.

Pause claims

For when the MCR cannot pay

Closes the claim form for everybody and says why. Claims already in the system are untouched, and approving and paying carry on.

Balances

What the MCR actually holds

The opening balance is what the account held before any disbursement arrived. Every payment you record under Planned costs is added on top of it, never in place of it, and everything paid out comes off. Cash in hand is counted separately.
