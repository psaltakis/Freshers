Roles are set on the server

People sign in with their own University account, and the address decides what they can do. Payment emails still go to the three shared committee inboxes below, so a change of treasurer does not change where anything lands. Both lists live in the Apps Script file, not in this page: to change either, edit ROLE_PEOPLE at the top of Code.gs and redeploy.

The inbox is where the treasury's mail goes and stays the same year to year. The people are the personal accounts allowed to sign in as that role, and change every handover. Several per role, comma separated.

Pause claims

For when the MCR cannot pay

Balances

What the MCR actually holds

The opening balance is what the account held before any disbursement arrived. Every payment you record under Planned costs is added on top of it, never in place of it, and everything paid out comes off. Cash in hand is counted separately.
